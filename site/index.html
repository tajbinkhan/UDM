The free download manager that works like IDM, on Windows, macOS and Linux

UDM (Unified Download Manager) fetches each file over up to 16 connections, resumes after interruptions, catches downloads from your browser and saves streamed videos as one file. Open source, with no ads, no trial and no sign-up.

What UDM does

Faster downloads

Each file is fetched in parts over up to 16 connections at once, or as streams of one connection when the server speaks HTTP/2. FTP works too.

Resume anything

Downloads pick up after a crash, a restart or a dropped connection. New link gives an expired download a fresh address and carries on.

Browser capture

The extension hands downloads from Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera and Firefox to UDM with their cookies, so files behind a sign-in work.

Video downloader

HLS, DASH and HDS streams in the quality you pick, with audio tracks and subtitles, joined into one file. YouTube, Vimeo and more through yt-dlp.

Queues and scheduler

Download overnight: queues start on a schedule, can wake the computer, and put it to sleep or shut it down when they're done.

Many files at once

Numbered patterns like photo[001-120].jpg, pasted lists, every link on a page, or a site grabber that crawls a site for the files you want.

Keeps going

A background program owns the downloads, so they continue after you close the browser and the window.

Scripts and AI assistants

IDM's command-line switches, a udm-cli tool, a versioned automation API, and an MCP server for Claude, Cursor and VS Code.

Speed limits and proxies

Limit all downloads or one, set a quota, and work through system, PAC, HTTP, HTTPS and SOCKS proxies with site logins.

UDM and Internet Download Manager

Nothing new to learn: the browser hands its downloads over, a save-as dialog asks where they go, and the categories, queues, scheduler and download windows are where you expect them.

Questions

Is UDM free?

Yes. UDM is free and open source under the MIT License. There is no trial, no serial number, no account and no ads.

Is there an IDM alternative for Mac and Linux?

UDM works the way Internet Download Manager does, with the same save-as dialog, categories, queues, scheduler and download windows, and it runs on Windows, macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Linux.

Which browsers does UDM work with?

UDM's extension catches downloads from Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera and Firefox, with their cookies and referrer, so files behind a sign-in work too.

Can UDM download videos?

Yes. It downloads HLS, DASH and HDS streams in the quality you pick and joins them into one file with ffmpeg, and it reads YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and Vimeo pages with yt-dlp.

Does a download resume after a crash or restart?

Yes. UDM resumes after a crash, a restart or a dropped connection without starting over, and its downloads keep going after the browser is closed.
