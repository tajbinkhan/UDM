**Release type:** patch

The browser extension no longer reports an error from tabs that were open when it was updated or reloaded.

## Fixed

- After the extension was updated or reloaded, a tab that was already open kept running its old copy of the extension's page script, which had lost its link to the extension. Playing a video there made it report **Uncaught (in promise) Error: Extension context invalidated** on the browser's extensions page, and clicking a link UDM would catch could do nothing. Now that old copy hides its video and selection buttons and leaves link clicks to the browser. (#317, 5ba0967)
