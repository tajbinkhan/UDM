**Release type:** patch

Stop, Pause and Resume work again for videos UDM downloads from YouTube and similar sites, and a minimized download window comes back when you ask for it.

## Fixed

- On Linux, **Stop**, **Pause** and **Cancel** did not stop a video downloading from a site such as YouTube: the video kept downloading and its row stayed "Downloading", so it could not be deleted either. Now the download stops at once, with nothing left running. (#327, 434355e)
- **Resume** started a paused video again from the beginning, leaving what it had already fetched on the disk. Now it carries on from where it stopped. (#327, 434355e)
- A download window that was minimized could not be brought back: double-clicking its download did nothing. Now double-clicking the download, or pressing Enter on it, brings its window back, and clicking UDM's tray icon or **Open UDM** in its menu brings download windows back with the main window. (#327, 434355e)
- On Linux, a video with a long name made its download window much wider than usual. Now the window keeps its size, and a long name in its title ends in "…". (#327, 434355e)
