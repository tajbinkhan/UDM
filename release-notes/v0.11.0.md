**Release type:** minor

The browser extension's video button now sits outside the video, and its popup lists only the downloads you can pause or resume.

## Changed

- The **Download this video** button now sits just above the video's top right corner, outside the picture, instead of over it. When the video starts at the very top of the window, the button goes just inside the video's top edge. The button stays while the pointer is on it. (#325, 09283ef)
- The extension's popup lists only downloads that are running or queued, each with **Pause**, and paused ones, each with **Resume**. Finished, failed and waiting downloads are in UDM's window. (#325, 09283ef)

## Fixed

- Turning the video button off for a site in the extension's popup, or everywhere in the popup or in Options, only took effect on a page after it was reloaded. Now the button goes away, or comes back, on pages already open. (#325, 09283ef)
