**Release type:** patch

YouTube downloads start sooner, keep their names in any language and show each stream's progress, and a click in the browser is no longer lost after UDM's background service restarts.

## Fixed

- A video whose title has letters Windows' code page lacks, such as Korean, was saved under a working name (`… .udm-45.mp4`), with an empty file at the chosen name that the download list pointed to. Now the video is saved under the chosen name, with every letter. (#348, c032f0d)
- **Show details** in the download window of a video from YouTube and other video sites showed an empty table. Now it shows a row for the video stream and one for its sound, with how much of each is in and whether it is connecting, receiving or finished. A finished connection of any download said "Retrying..."; now it says "Finished". (#348, c032f0d)
- A YouTube download started about 8 seconds after **Start Download**, because UDM read the video's page again and then waited out the time YouTube asks for before a video it shows an ad for. Now UDM reads the page when the menu or Download File Info opens and the download starts from that read, so the first bytes come within about 2 seconds once you have spent a few seconds on the dialog. (#348, c032f0d)
- The **Download this video** bar did not appear over a video that started playing by itself, as YouTube's do, until you paused and played it. Now it appears on such a video too. (#348, c032f0d)
- When UDM's background service (`udm-daemon`) restarted while the browser stayed open, after an update or a crash, the next thing sent from the browser was lost, such as a click on a quality in the video menu. Now the browser's connection to UDM reconnects and sends it once. (#347, 3bf400e)

## Install

Pick the file for your system from the list below. The [README](https://github.com/tajbinkhan/UDM#download) has the full steps.

The files are not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" the first time. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. The [code signing policy](https://github.com/tajbinkhan/UDM#code-signing-policy) says how release files are built and who approves them.

The macOS disk image is experimental. It builds and passes CI on macOS, but it has not been run on a real Mac yet, so please [open an issue](https://github.com/tajbinkhan/UDM/issues) for anything that goes wrong.
