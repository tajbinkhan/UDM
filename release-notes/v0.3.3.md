**Release type:** patch

This release fixes two problems with UIDM's windows on Linux under Wayland.

## Fixed

- On Linux under Wayland, closing the window to the tray left it frozen on screen with a "Not Responding" message. Closing it now takes the window off the screen and leaves UIDM in the tray; the tray's Open UIDM, starting UIDM again, or a download that needs its save-as dialog brings it back. (46807ec)
- On Linux under Wayland, dialogs kept the size they opened at, so a wider one, such as the tip of the day, lost its right edge and its Close button. Dialogs fit what they show now. (ecd0749)
- On Linux, UIDM started at login now waits in the tray instead of opening a minimized window. (46807ec)
