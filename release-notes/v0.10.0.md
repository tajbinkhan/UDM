**Release type:** minor

**Update now** shows how far the new version's download has come, in About and in the main window's update notice.

## New

- While **Update now** downloads the new version, **About** shows a bar and how much has arrived, such as **12.3 MB of 40.0 MB (30%)**, then **Checking and installing...** once all of it is there. Before, it said **Downloading the update...** and nothing more until UDM closed. A version UDM has already downloaded in the background installs at once, without a bar. (#322, 9efbc6b)
- The update notice under the main window's toolbar shows the same bar and text in place of its **Update now** button while the download runs. (#322, 9efbc6b)
